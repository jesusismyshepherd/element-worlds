# Ideas for the next worlds (wings)

**Moved to the studio on 2026-10-29.** Every idea below is now an item in `docs/studio/backlog.json`,
which the user sees and steers in the studio; add and change ideas there, not here. This file keeps the
first brainstorm (2026-10-27) as it was. How to build a wing: `.claude/skills/element-wing/` and
`docs/knowledge/adding-a-wing.md`.

## Shared worlds (one wing serves a whole family)

- **The neon sign workshop** (He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn): fill glass tubes with each noble gas and switch
  them on; every gas glows its own colour (helium peach, neon red-orange, argon violet, krypton white,
  xenon blue). Bend tubes into letters and write your name. Scientist: emission spectra through a prism.
  **Built 2026-10-28** (`/e/<Symbol>/signs`, `src/worlds/wings/noble/`): the filling bench, radon's lead
  box, your name in neon, the spectroscope (NIST lines under the Sun's 1868 line), the atom and its
  energy ladder, the history board (1898, 1910, today) and the balloons.
- **The magnet and motor workshop** (Fe, Co, Ni, Nd, Sm, Dy): build an electric motor; compare iron,
  ferrite and neodymium magnets lifting weights; why wind turbines and electric cars need rare earths.
- **The particle accelerator** (Z 104 to 118): fire a calcium beam at a target and, maybe once a week,
  make one atom of a new element that lasts a thousandth of a second. The island of stability.

## Single elements

- **Hydrogen**: inside the Sun's core (four hydrogens become helium, light comes out); a hydrogen car
  whose exhaust is water; the lightest gas lifting a balloon.
- **Helium**: superfluid helium creeping up the walls of its cup; the 1868 eclipse where it was found in
  the Sun's light before anyone found it on Earth.
- **Lithium**: a battery factory (lithium-ion cells charging a scooter); the lightest metal floating on oil.
- **Nitrogen**: a liquid nitrogen lab (−196 °C: a flower that shatters, instant ice cream); the nitrogen
  cycle in the soil, with the bacteria on bean roots.
- **Sodium and chlorine**: Aveiro's salt pans; table salt crystals growing; why sodium must never touch
  water (watched behind glass).
- **Magnesium**: fireworks and the green heart of chlorophyll.
- **Aluminium**: from bauxite to a can and back (recycling saves about 95% of the energy).
- **Silicon**: from sand to a chip; walk inside a processor where the transistors are buildings.
- **Phosphorus**: the match factory; the backbone of DNA; Hennig Brand's glowing flask (1669).
- **Sulfur**: a volcano crater with yellow crystals.
- **Calcium**: a coral reef and a limestone cave with stalactites growing; your skeleton.
- **Iron**: the blacksmith's forge (heat, hammer, quench: steel); a journey to the Earth's core; red blood.
- **Copper**: the electricity hall (wires, a dynamo, lights); the Statue of Liberty turning green.
- **Silver**: the photographer's darkroom; the shiniest mirror.
- **Gold**: the treasure vault and gold leaf thinner than a hair's width; born in colliding neutron stars.
- **Mercury**: the thermometer and the liquid metal that a steel ball floats on.
- **Uranium**: the reactor's control room, a chain-reaction game (catch the neutrons with control rods).
- **Titanium**: a jet engine and a hip implant.
- **Tungsten**: the light bulb factory.

## Lab extensions (the molecule games)

- **Ionic compounds**: a metal hands its electrons over (NaCl, MgO, CaCl2), crystals grow from the ions.
  **Built 2026-10-28** in the Molecule Lab (`molecule-lab.md`); the growing crystals not yet.
- **Alloys**: the forge where metals mix (bronze, brass, steel: iron with a little carbon).
- **Reactions**: burning methane with oxygen (atoms counted on both sides), rusting, photosynthesis.
  Methane and rust are benches in oxygen's fire wing, photosynthesis is carbon's tree; as a game in the
  lab, not yet.
- **Rings and life**: benzene, glucose, the four DNA bases; build a short strand of DNA.
